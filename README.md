## The pitch

> We breed petunias that glow in the dark so your garden lights itself at night, because homeowners already spend a fortune on outdoor lamps that rust, short and die in the rain.

176 characters. Ridiculous at first glance — a plant as a lamp — real by the
second: homeowners already buy outdoor lighting that rusts, shorts and dies
in the rain, and a self-lighting petunia replaces it. The sentence names the
odd thing being sold (glowing petunias), who pays for it (homeowners) and why
it holds together (it replaces the lamps they already buy). Glow-in-the-dark
petunias are a real product: bioluminescent petunias are sold to gardeners
today.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only, no third-party imports) writes the
sentence to `/output/entry.txt`. The output is deterministic — byte-identical
on every run.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
